# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

## Real-Time Psychrometric-Based Decision Support System for Greenhouse Environmental Management

You are an autonomous senior software engineer, scientific computing engineer, agricultural/environmental control engineer, and research-oriented AI development agent.

Your task is to independently design, implement, test, validate, document, and finalize a complete research-grade application for greenhouse environmental monitoring, psychrometric analysis, and rule-based decision support.

You are expected to work continuously and autonomously through all development stages until the project is in a stable, usable, documented state.

Do not stop after creating a prototype.

Do not wait for confirmation for every small implementation decision.

When a reasonable engineering decision can be made from the requirements below, make the decision yourself, document it, and continue.

The final system must be reproducible, technically defensible, scientifically structured, and suitable to support a master's thesis and future journal publications.

---

The research project concerns greenhouse environmental management using real-time environmental sensor data and psychrometric analysis.

The overall research concept is:

Sensor Data
→ Environmental Data Processing
→ Psychrometric Calculation
→ Environmental State Assessment
→ Decision Support System
→ Management Recommendation
→ Optional Human/Operator Action

The master's-level core is:

REAL-TIME ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT
+
PSYCHROMETRIC ANALYSIS
+
RULE-BASED DECISION SUPPORT
+
MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATION

AI/ML prediction and fully autonomous closed-loop control should remain extensible future work.

# 3. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES

The final system should support the following research objectives:

1. Acquire greenhouse environmental data in real time.
2. Process temperature and relative humidity measurements.
3. Calculate scientifically valid psychrometric parameters.
4. Visualize the environmental state using a psychrometric chart.
5. Provide Mollier h-x visualization where appropriate.
6. Determine the current greenhouse environmental state.
7. Identify conditions that may require management intervention.
8. Provide rule-based management recommendations.
9. Log environmental data and system decisions.
10. Allow historical data analysis.
11. Evaluate whether environmental conditions move toward target conditions after management intervention.
12. Provide sufficient logging and reproducibility for scientific analysis.

# 14. VPD

VPD should be treated as a plant-relevant environmental indicator derived from temperature and humidity.

The system should calculate VPD consistently and transparently.

Do not create arbitrary VPD formulas without documentation.

The DSS should be able to use VPD as one of the decision variables.

However:

Do not make VPD the only decision variable.

The system should preserve the broader psychrometric state.
